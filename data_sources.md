## Data Sources

### MarineTraffic
MarineTraffic provides AIS vessel metadata and limited access to trajectory-level data under free plans.
Access to historical, high-resolution AIS position reports appears to require a commercial subscription.

### Public AIS Dataset (2024)
To assess feasibility before committing to commercial data, a publicly available AIS dataset
covering 2024 vessel tracks was obtained in GeoPackage (.gpkg) format.

Initial inspection confirms the file is a valid SQLite-based GeoPackage. Further integrity checks
and recovery are in progress due to reported database corruption.
